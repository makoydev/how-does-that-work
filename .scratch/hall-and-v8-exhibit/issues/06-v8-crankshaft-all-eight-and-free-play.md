# 06: V8 Exhibit: crankshaft, all eight, and Free Play

**What to build:** Steps six and seven complete the Walkthrough: the camera moves to the crankshaft to show a connecting rod turning up-and-down motion into rotation, then all eight cylinders run with the firing order highlighted so the Visitor sees why the crank spins smoothly. After the last Step, Free Play unlocks three plainly labelled controls: an RPM slider that drives the crank speed, a cutaway toggle that hides the near half of the block, and a follow-one-cylinder button that slows the engine and locks the camera on cylinder one. Leaving Free Play returns to the last Step.
