# 07: Mini models on pedestals

**What to build:** An Exhibit may provide a small 3D model for its pedestal. When it does, the Hall places the model above the placard so the Visitor can recognise the subject from across the room; when it does not, the pedestal shows the placard only. The V8 Exhibit provides a mini engine made of its block and crankshaft, scaled to fit the pedestal.

**Blocked by:** 05 V8 Exhibit: the parts and the four strokes

**Status:** ready-for-agent

- [ ] The Exhibit contract's mini model is optional and the registry accepts Exhibits with or without one
- [ ] A pedestal with no mini model shows only the placard
- [ ] A pedestal with a mini model shows it above the placard at a size that fits the pedestal
- [ ] The V8 pedestal shows a mini engine that is recognisable as the same subject as the Exhibit
- [ ] Highlighting on look-at still works on pedestals with mini models
